mushroom

A domed cap with an overhanging rim above a stout, round-based stem. The cap's concave underside is what makes it read unmistakably as a fungus.

Construction · 24 × 24 grid

The cap

One closed path: a radius-8 dome on top, with an underside that bows upward in the middle. That bow leaves the rim corners as the lowest points, so the cap drapes over the stem.

The stem

A stout, round-based stem. Its walls run up just far enough to meet the cap's underside — the ends finish inside the cap stroke, so no tip shows in the hollow.

Reads as fungus

An umbrella's canopy is convex underneath with a thin handle. Inverting that curve — concave underside, stout stem — is the cue that makes this a mushroom.

    Two paths, all integers

    An arc plus a quadratic curve for the cap, and the stem — no transforms, fills, or explicit strokes. Every coordinate is a whole unit, so nothing needs rounding and the icon stays crisp on the pixel grid.
