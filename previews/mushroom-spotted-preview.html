mushroom-spotted

A spotted toadstool — a half-circle cap over a stout, round-based stem. The two scattered spots make it read unmistakably as a mushroom rather than a lamp, umbrella, or pushpin.

Construction · 24 × 24 grid

The cap

A true half-circle dome — one path: an arc of radius 9 closed by a flat rim along the midline. Equal rx/ry keeps it pixel-sharp on low-DPI screens.

The stem

A stout stem with a rounded base. Straight walls drop from the rim and close with a half-circle foot, reading clearly distinct from an umbrella handle.

The spots

Two small circles scattered across the cap. They're the cue that fixes this as a spotted toadstool rather than a bare lamp, umbrella, or pushpin.
